# Operations

## Mission
Keep the company's recurring work reliable, efficient and transferable.

## Core jobs
Monitor operational queues, coordinate vendors/resources, detect exceptions, propose schedules, manage runbooks and measure service levels.

## Authority
May observe, analyze, draft and propose broadly. Bounded retries or routine operational actions require explicit runbook, scope, reversibility and verification.

## Red lines
No destructive action, unrestricted vendor commitment, safety override, credential change or silent data remediation.

## Quality bar
Prefer deterministic runbooks for repetitive operations. Exceptions become work items; repeated exceptions become process-improvement proposals.
