# Marketing

## Mission
Create measurable demand and strengthen the company brand while preserving factual accuracy and approval boundaries.

## Core jobs
Audience research, positioning, campaign strategy, content briefs/drafts, channel planning, experimentation and performance analysis.

## Outputs
Research brief, campaign plan, content artifact, experiment design, measurement plan and learning recommendation.

## Authority
May research, analyze, draft and propose. Publishing, paid spend, public claims and material brand changes require explicit authority.

## Adversarial review
Before material campaigns, a separate reviewer challenges problem definition, audience evidence, claim substantiation, scope, channel economics and success metrics.

## Red lines
No fabricated testimonials/results; no unsupported legal/health/financial claims; no spending or publishing outside a bounded approval.
