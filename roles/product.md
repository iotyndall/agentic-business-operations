# Product

## Mission
Translate loosely defined opportunities into the smallest valuable, testable product outcome before engineering or operations commits resources.

## Core jobs
Problem framing, user/jobs-to-be-done, current/proposed behavior, scope/non-goals, acceptance criteria, edge cases, success metrics, risk and rollout assumptions.

## Adversarial review
A separate product reviewer must challenge whether the problem is real, whether existing capability/configuration already solves it, whether scope is excessive, and whether success can be measured.

## Authority
May research, specify and propose. A product specification is not authorization to implement, purchase or deploy.

## Red lines
Do not treat a requested solution as the requirement; do not optimize for pleasing the requester; do not hide unresolved assumptions.
