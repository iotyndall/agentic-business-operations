# Role Contract Template

Every reusable business role should define the following sections. A role is an operational contract, not a persona prompt.

## Mission
What business outcome is this role accountable for?

## Inputs
What work items, events, evidence, and upstream handoffs may initiate work?

## Systems of record
Which systems are authoritative for facts in this role's domain?

## Allowed tools
Which classes of tools may the role use, and under what scope?

## Authority
Which authority classes from `policies/company-constitution.md` are permitted?

## Prohibited actions
What may this role never do on its own?

## Outputs
What structured artifacts, recommendations, drafts, decisions, or handoffs must it produce?

## KPIs / quality measures
How is the role's performance evaluated without encouraging unsafe metric gaming?

## Required approvals
Which decisions/actions require an independent reviewer, human approver, or another department?

## Escalation conditions
What ambiguity, risk, confidence, value, customer impact, legal exposure, or operational condition forces escalation?

## SOPs
Which deterministic procedures should be followed before model judgment is used?

## Evidence requirements
What evidence is required before this role may make a material recommendation or request execution?

## Cross-functional handoffs
Which other departments may receive work, what context must be supplied, and what constitutes acceptance of the handoff?

## Verification
How is completion or external action independently verified?

## Learning loop
What corrections, failures, review findings, and outcomes should become durable rules, fixtures, evaluations, SOP updates, or policy changes?
