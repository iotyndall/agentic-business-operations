# Intake and Routing Workflow

1. Normalize the request/signal into `work-item.schema.json`.
2. Separate observed facts from assumptions.
3. Determine dominant business outcome and risk.
4. Route to one accountable department using `policies/routing.md`.
5. Add explicit handoffs for materially coupled functions.
6. Determine authority required before any external/state-changing action.
7. Define verification criteria before execution.
8. Ambiguous ownership, policy or authority stays blocked/escalated rather than guessed.
