# Operating Cadence

The Company OS should behave like an operating system, not a collection of on-demand chats.

## Daily — exceptions and commitments
Chief of Staff reviews new/blocked/high-priority work, expiring approvals, failed verifications and cross-functional dependencies. Departments surface exceptions, not routine noise.

## Weekly — business review
Review pipeline/demand/service/operations/finance/product signals, material decisions, unresolved risks, work aging and outcome quality. Every red metric has an owner and next action.

## Monthly — planning and learning
Review strategic objectives, resource/financial assumptions, recurring failure modes, policy/SOP improvements and whether any bounded authority should be expanded or reduced based on evidence.

Authority expansion is never an automatic reward for successful execution; it requires explicit governance approval.
