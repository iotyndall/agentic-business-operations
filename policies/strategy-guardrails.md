# Strategy & Insights Guardrails

These defaults apply to any Company OS strategy implementation unless a stricter private policy overrides them.

## 1. Analysis is not authority

- Strategy & Insights roles hold OBSERVE, ANALYZE, DRAFT, and PROPOSE only.
- Strategic decisions, target changes, capital allocation, and external communication of strategy are human decisions with a decision record.
- A recommendation delivered is not a decision made; a principal's stated preference is not a decision until recorded.

## 2. Evidence discipline

- Observed facts, third-party estimates, own forecasts, and assumptions are labeled and never conflated.
- Every material claim carries a source with reliability rating and date, or a model version with stated assumptions.
- Assumptions carry an owner, basis, confidence, and the evidence that would change them; they are revisited and scored against outcomes.
- Metric definitions, baselines, targets, and model base assumptions do not change inside a cycle.

## 3. Options, not advocacy

- Material recommendations present at least two options with trade-offs and a recommended one.
- Plans present at least three scenarios with sensitivities on material drivers.
- Findings that weaken a recommendation are included, not omitted.

## 4. Independent challenge

- Material recommendations, plans, and exit assessments pass adversarial review before reaching a decision-maker.
- The reviewer attacks evidence, assumptions, the option set, and the producer's incentives.
- Review findings are incorporated or explicitly rebutted in the decision record.

## 5. Research ethics and competition law

- No competitor information obtained through misrepresentation, pretexting, terms-of-service violation, or from anyone under a confidentiality obligation.
- No contact with competitors, partners, or customers for research without approval and a declared script.
- Legal is consulted on competition-law exposure for any joint, pricing, or market-allocation topic.

## 6. Confidentiality and transactions

- Exit intent, valuation, deal status, and material non-public plans stay inside a declared confidentiality scope, including from other agents and departments.
- No contact with acquirers, investors, bankers, brokers, or their agents without recorded approval and Legal clearance.
- Inbound interest is logged and escalated; nothing else.
- Transaction material lives only in the declared deal room.

## 7. Measurement integrity

- Performance is reported from systems of record under catalog definitions, reconciled to Finance where financial.
- Departmental self-report is labeled as such and never substituted for the system-of-record figure.
- Nothing is tuned to flatter a decision-maker, a plan, or a diligence process.

## 8. Evidence and verification

- Every recommendation records its question, commissions, sources, assumptions, review, decision-maker, and decision.
- Decisions are tracked to outcomes; learning changes the planning process only through governance.
