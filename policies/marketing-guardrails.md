# Marketing Guardrails

These defaults apply to any Company OS marketing implementation unless a stricter private policy overrides them. They define control categories, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## 1. Least privilege and capability binding

- Marketing roles receive named capabilities, not arbitrary platform or list access.
- Every capability maps to a declared private system and a bounded audience, channel, budget, and content scope.
- Undeclared channels, accounts, platforms, or lists fail closed.

## 2. Claims and controlled content

- Material product, price, offer, result, legal, health, or financial statements come from the approved claims register or an accountable source department.
- The Brand Manager authors the standard; the Marketing Content & Claims Reviewer gates against it; neither is the sole approver of its own work.
- A clearance applies only to the exact version reviewed.

## 3. Consent, suppression, and audience data

- Email and message sends require recorded consent and a suppression check as a deterministic prerequisite.
- Testimonials, reviews, photos, and likeness require a recorded consent or license before publication.
- Audience data is retrieved and used only within declared fields and purposes; no purchased or scraped lists.

## 4. Budget and spend

- Finance owns spend authority; marketing roles consume an approved envelope and may pause spend at any time.
- Creating budget, increasing an envelope, adding a platform, or extending geography requires approval.
- Spend is verified against billed amounts and the company ledger, not configured budgets.

## 5. Targeting and platform policy

- No targeting or exclusion on protected characteristics or proxies for them.
- Platform policy rejections, warnings, or brand-safety incidents pause the affected activity and require review before resumption.
- No fabricated engagement, fraudulent inventory, or undisclosed paid promotion.

## 6. Public interaction boundary

- Social replies and DMs that concern service, safety, fraud, legal, press, or regulators are escalated, not answered in channel.
- Customer matters are handed to Service as structured work items; marketing roles do not resolve or promise.
- During a declared crisis or incident window, publishing is approval-required across all channels.

## 7. Agent-facing surfaces and counterpart agents

- External AI agents are proxies for humans or organizations; every consent, suppression, claims, and approval control applies to the agent exactly as to its principal.
- Agent-facing surfaces (Agent Cards, catalogs, manifests, structured facts, `llms.txt`) are controlled content: reviewed before publication, version-exact, traceable attribute-by-attribute to the claims register.
- Embedding instructions or adversarial content in data intended to manipulate a counterpart agent is prohibited.
- Negotiation with a counterpart agent is approval-required unless a declared envelope pre-authorizes it; negotiation output is a proposal until an authorized role executes.
- Registrations on protocols, registries, and marketplaces are declared in the private contract; undeclared ones fail closed.

## 8. Measurement integrity

- Attribution windows, conversion definitions, and reporting baselines are declared in the private contract and not changed to improve apparent performance.
- Reach, engagement, volume, or efficiency never override consent, claims, targeting, or approval controls.

## 9. Evidence and verification

- Every send, spend, and publish records brief version, claims trace, approval, and platform confirmation.
- Execution is verified against the platform's reported state, not the tool call.
- Repeated review findings become guideline, register, or routing changes through governance, never silent workarounds.
