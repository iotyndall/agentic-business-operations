# Separation of Duties

High autonomy does not imply high authority.

For high-risk work, no single role may author the recommendation, approve it, execute it and verify it.

Minimum boundaries:
- proposal author cannot be the independent reviewer;
- reviewer cannot silently edit the reviewed artifact and still call the review independent;
- an execution agent cannot grant its own authority;
- material financial/legal/security/customer-data actions require a separately authorized approver;
- verification evidence must refer to the actual executed action/outcome, not the proposal.

When role independence cannot be established, result is `ESCALATE`.
